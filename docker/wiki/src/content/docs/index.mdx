The Legend Motor Company Integrated System is a WinForms-based platform for order processing, inventory control, customer records, and invoicing.

Core modules:

- Order management
- Spare part management
- Stock management
- User management
- Invoice management

This Starlight wiki is the active documentation site for local development and operations.
